Production

Our main asset is a large production facility of our own, where we process cotton and make all our products.

Our facility is staffed by experienced specialists. Every stage — from raw material intake to packing finished goods — takes place on one site under our control.

We regularly maintain and upgrade our equipment to produce consistent quality and deliver large orders on time.
