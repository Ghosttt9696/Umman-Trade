From raw cotton to finished goods

The entire processing cycle takes place at our site.

  1. 01

    Raw material intake

    Selection and inspection of seed cotton for quality and moisture.

  2. 02

    Cleaning

    Removal of trash and impurities, separating fibre from seeds.

  3. 03

    Processing

    Producing fibre, linter and fluff; carding and spinning yarn.

  4. 04

    Inspection

    Every batch is checked against key parameters.

  5. 05

    Packing

    Pressing into bales, winding yarn onto cones, labelling.

  6. 06

    Shipping

    Loading and dispatch to the customer on the agreed date.

Full-cycle production

A large facility of our own, equipped for cleaning, processing and spinning cotton. We control every stage, so we can stand behind quality and lead times.
