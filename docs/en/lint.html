Cotton linter

Short cotton fibre for the cellulose, cotton wool and textile industries.

Linter is the short fibre left on cotton seeds after ginning. It is a valuable cellulose-rich raw material used to make cotton wool, paper, cellulose and nonwovens.

Advantages

  • High cellulose content
  • Minimal impurities
  • Dense pressing for efficient transport
